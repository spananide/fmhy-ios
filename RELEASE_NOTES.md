First release of FMHY for iOS (iOS 17+).

**Install:** download the `.ipa` below and sideload it with [Sideloadly](https://sideloadly.io) or SideStore/AltStore. Step by step instructions are in the [README](https://github.com/spananide/fmhy-ios#install-free-no-mac-needed).

- Whole wiki offline, refreshable
- Typo-tolerant search with filters
- Bookmarks, link checker, Safari view

Official iOS port of FMHY for Android (omeg4-dev); a community app, not an official FMHY project. Feedback: [open an issue](https://github.com/spananide/fmhy-ios/issues/new/choose).
